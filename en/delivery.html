Delivery & Shipping Policy

Hong Kong Fresh Flower Delivery · Worldwide Dried & Preserved Flower Shipping

🌿 Hong Kong Local Delivery

  • Delivery hours: every day 10:00–18:00, courier delivery
  • Free delivery: orders over HK$500 — free delivery to Hong Kong Island / Kowloon / New Territories urban areas (no time slot)
  • Outlying islands: HK$300 surcharge
  • Same-day / urgent delivery & time slots: additional charge (as shown at checkout)
  • We deliver to homes, offices, malls, schools, hotels and Hong Kong International Airport (non-restricted areas only)
  • Fresh flowers last approx. 5–7 days, depending on environment, climate and temperature

Final shipping fee is calculated in real time at checkout.

International orders support Credit Card / Apple Pay / Google Pay only (no cash or FPS). Select your country at checkout and the shipping fee is calculated automatically.
