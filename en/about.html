About Us — Luxury Floral Art: Preserved Flowers & Everlasting Blooms

RoyalSpl Florist is a Hong Kong luxury florist renowned for same-day fresh flower delivery, wedding florals and corporate gifting. Aetheria Botanica, our preserved flowers and everlasting blooms brand, creates artisanal floral decor for the home — delivered worldwide.

We believe flowers are more than gifts — they are a way of living. From fresh bouquets delivered across Hong Kong to preserved flowers and everlasting blooms shipped across the globe, we craft each arrangement in a minimalist, luxurious style that brings the beauty of nature into every celebration and every living space.

Fresh Flowers · Hong Kong
